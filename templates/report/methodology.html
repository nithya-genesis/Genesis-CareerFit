Methodology & Glossary
Algorithmic scoring principles and metric interpretations
SECTION 06

CareerFit Index: A normalized composite score measuring technical domain depth, situational behavioral maturity, academic tracking, and branch alignment.

Evidence Confidence: Indicates dataset completeness. High confidence indicates multiple validated assessment submissions, whereas Insufficient Evidence designates unassessed domains.

Zero Hallucination Guarantee: All numerical indicators in this report are computed strictly through deterministic algorithmic routines.
